One image that sums up the world news of **Tuesday 24 November 2026**: a flat editorial front page, "THE WORLD TODAY", drawn as HTML/CSS/SVG and rendered to a 1024×1024 JPEG (stored as `entry.png`). Under a masthead naming the day, six stories carry the date:

- **World Court** — the ICJ rules Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, a first in the court's history, as 14 nations back the ruling.
- **Climate** — the COP31 talks in Antalya collapse without a deal after 90+ nations reject a draft that never mentioned "fossil fuels".
- **War at sea** — Kyiv says its naval drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury off Benghazi, Libya; the UN seeks a probe.
- **Business** — Nvidia reinstates CEO Jensen Huang after Trump's intervention; the US blacklists chip firm Megaspeed and markets surge.
- **Security** — NATO vows defence against space threats amid reports Russia may orbit a nuclear-armed satellite within 60 days.
- **Americas** — Mexico captures cartel boss "El Chapo Isidro" with US drone help and will try him at home, not extradite him.

An "Also Today" column adds the UN chief's call for global AI risk rules, Nepal's magnitude 5.6 earthquake, deadly strikes in Gaza, and Bangladesh's 50-million-child typhoid vaccination drive. `main.ts` copies the shipped image to the output directory, byte for byte, on every run.

node main.ts
